About Goker

Online, Göker Cebeci publishes his work under the name Goker. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and a bachelor's degree in Graphic Design. His practice moves between research, code, visual systems and photography. The subjects change, but the method remains consistent: examine a system, make its structure visible, and build something that can be tested, reused or experienced.

Goker's academic research focuses on machine learning, information retrieval and the behavior of search engines. His work studies how semantic relevance, technical performance, accessibility and interface design affect ranking and navigation. He also develops reproducible research tools, datasets and applied artificial-intelligence systems.

As an artist and designer, Goker works where code meets concept. His generative projects use artificial intelligence, art history and rule-based systems as creative material. His photographic practice is more personal, exploring mood, memory and the emotional narratives carried by images.

He lives and works in a multigenerational family home in Edirne, surrounded by a dense garden, animals and an ongoing collection of practical experiments. This environment connects his technical and creative work through a persistent do-it-yourself approach: build, observe, repair and rebuild.
